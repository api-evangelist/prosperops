The article explains how automation addresses the challenge of optimizing cloud costs at scale across multi-cloud environments. Manual processes cannot keep pace with dynamic cloud infrastructure changes, resulting in missed savings opportunities. The piece provides a three-step framework for launching automated cost optimization programs and highlights key metrics like Effective Savings Rate and Commitment Lock-in Risk for measuring automation's impact on return on investment.
